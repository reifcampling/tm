Collector Tickets

Make your entrance to the FORMULA 1 AUSTRALIAN GRAND PRIX 2027 truly unforgettable with a stylish Collector Ticket. Not only will these tickets grant you access to the event, but they also serve as a cherished keepsake to remember for years to come. Please note that Collector Tickets can only be purchased with an Australian Postal address, so make sure your Ticketmaster account details are up to date. Expected delivery - 4 weeks prior to the event. Collector Tickets incur an additional $25 fee per ticket.

What are Aisle Seat Tickets?

Looking to add some added comfort to your race day? We've got you covered with Aisle Seat Tickets!

Aisle seats are the closest 4 seats to each aisle and cost an additional $25. This is also reflected in the seats at the back of an aisle which do not have any seats in front of them.
