Frequently Asked Questions

What's my member presale password?
Ticketed Club Members will receive their unique AAN (Advantage Access Number) password on Friday 4th September; they will then receive an email at 10:00am Monday with a link to enter their AAN password to access tickets.

Non-Ticketed Club Members will receive an email from their Club at 4:00pm Monday with a password to access their pre-sale.
Can I purchase the same seat as my Club membership?
Same seat allocation is only available at certain venues due to capacity and ticketing agent constraints. If same seat is available for your Club's home Finals game/s, you will receive an email with additional information prior to the presale. For any other games, tickets can be purchased by members through the normal presale window
