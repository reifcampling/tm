FAQ’s | On Sale

When do tickets general access and hospitality tickets go on sale?
Tickets for the 2026 NFL Melbourne Game are now on sale. Find and purchase tickets here.
Are there travel and accommodation packages?
Ticket + Hotel packages are available through official partner Sportsnet Holidays.
What is the difference between General Access and Hospitality?
General Access is an allocated seat only. Standing room from $95, General Tickets from $140.

Hospitality includes a premium seat plus food and beverages at one of the MCG's iconic hospitality venues. Hospitality packages start from $1,600 per person.
When and where is the game?
The NFL 2026 Melbourne Game will be on Friday 11 September 2026 for a 10.35AM kick-off. Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG). Gates open at 7AM on game day.
Which teams are playing?
San Francisco 49ers v Los Angeles Rams.
Are accessible tickets available?
Tickets can be purchased through the Ticketmaster Website, or by submitting a request through the Ticketmaster Help Centre following these steps:

1. From the dropdown menu, select Accessible Bookings as the topic.
2. Then select Los Angeles Rams v San Francisco 49ers as the event name.

Alternatively, you can call our Accessible Booking Line on 1300 446 925, available Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am - 5:00 pm AEDT. Submitting a request doesn't guarantee a ticket, availability and type of accessible tickets will vary by venue. A Ticketmaster representative will be in touch if further information is required.

For more information, visit our help pageAccessible Tickets: Everything you need to know.
What are the hospitality packages and where can I purchase them?
General Admission: up to 6 tickets per transaction

Hospitality packages: up to 10 tickets per transaction

General Access: up to 6 tickets per transaction. Hospitality: up to 10 tickets per transaction.
When will I receive my tickets?
After completing your purchase, your tickets will be available to manage via your Ticketmaster account. Please note that ticket barcodes will not be displayed until 30 days prior to the game.
Who are the official sellers?
All tickets are sold exclusively through Ticketmaster. Ticket + Hotel packages are available through official partner Sportsnet Holidays sportsnetholidays.com. If you are a fan travelling from the USA, On Location are the official hospitality partner of the NFL and you can view experience offerings here. Only purchase through official channels.
Will there be fan events for the 2026 NFL Melbourne Game?
Yes. The NFL Kickoff Festival presented by American Express will take over Melbourne Park from Thursday, September 10 to Saturday, September 12, bringing fans closer to the game with interactive NFL experiences, Flag Football, live entertainment, watch parties, retail pop-ups, an American food village, player and legend appearances, and more. Grounds Passes start from $49 for Thursday and Saturday sessions and $59 for Friday, with children aged 13 and under admitted free when redeemed with an accompanying adult ticket.
